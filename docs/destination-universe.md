# Destination discovery boundary

The curated `DESTINATION_UNIVERSE` contains canonical place identities, zh/en names, country and region hierarchy, entity type, traits, scenery category and possible access hubs. It is independent of Duffel's location resolver. Coordinates are null until a verified geographic dataset is integrated; they are never guessed.

1. AI may propose 10–16 names from the curated universe. The server validates each name against the universe and discards invented names or mismatched airport codes. A deterministic catalog provides up to 30 candidates when AI fails, and supplements the AI pool for ordinary discovery.
2. Hard transport/geography exclusions run before scoring. Explicit intent overlap contributes up to 40 points; inferred seasonal overlap up to 18; verified end-to-end flight and budget fit contribute at most 25; long-tail distinctiveness contributes 8; duration and transport preferences contribute separately. The general familiarity prior is capped at 2 points. No capital-status bonus exists. Missing budgets produce no affordability claim.
3. `rerankDestinations` applies deterministic penalties to repeated scenery, themes, countries, entity types and iconic picks. A current-session history subtracts 28 presentation points from recently shown destinations. Those penalties are 35% as strong when the user supplied a precise intent. Numeric fit scores are left unchanged. Refresh draws another three from the existing pool without contacting AI or providers.
4. `DestinationAccessResolver` supplies possible airport and rail hubs plus road-trip suitability. Duffel is called only for a hub already supported by the provider; at most eight diverse candidates are checked per live discovery. A verified hub flight with unverified onward travel is `partially_verified`. Its hub quote is kept apart from a complete destination quote and is not used for budget or total-price claims. No live rail or road fares, times or availability are inferred.
5. Presentation resolves the canonical entity to localized city/country names and taxonomy labels. Visuals follow city → region → scenery category → global illustration, without claiming an illustration depicts a particular attraction.

This is a curated starter catalog rather than a complete geographic database. Additional destinations, coordinates, hub relationships, and regional imagery can be added without expanding Duffel's allowlist; actual onward routing and full trip prices require future providers.
