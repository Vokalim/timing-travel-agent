# Provisional planning and preference fit

Origin is needed to compare transport. Destination, exact dates, duration, budgets and minimum rating may be omitted. `createTripRequest` preserves `destination: null`, raw preference text, travel intents and `constraints`. The same normalized request is available to discovery, flight, stay and future itinerary planners; never replace the user's broad period with a fabricated exact date.

`prepareExploration` derives at most three representative departure dates. Each has `source: system_generated_exploration_window` and `userProvided: false`. The original broad period stays in `departureWindowText` and `context.dateDescription`. When duration is absent, weekends use a provisional 2–3 night range; other trips use 4–6 nights, with the midpoint used for this comparison. Users can edit both later. A missing budget has `budgetStatus: unconfirmed`; the result describes value and convenience, not affordability.

`parsePreferenceConstraints` maps free text to `hard`, `strong`, `soft`, `pace`, and `rawText`. Hard constraints filter quotes and excluded destinations. Direct flights and red-eye wording distinguish mandatory (“只看直飞”, “不要红眼”) from preferred (“最好直飞”, “尽量不要红眼”). In the quote engine, the baseline value/quality score is scaled to 70 points, plus 10 points, with preference fit capped to ±30 points. Direct-flight preference contributes +16 or −12; daytime preference +16 or −16; fewer stops +8 to −8; shorter flights up to ±8; higher-rated stays up to +8; central stays +12; transport-convenient stays +10; evidenced soft signals up to +4 each. When both budgets are unset, general convenience adds +6 for a direct flight or up to −8 for stops, and ±3 for known daytime versus overnight timing. Total is clipped to 0–100. No LLM assigns a numeric score.

Discovery has a separate deterministic fit: explicit intent up to 40, seasonal fit up to 18, short-trip suitability 10, general familiarity 5, verified availability 10, nonstop 5, specified flight budget ±10, then explicit preference signals (direct ±16, red-eye ±16, scenery ±12, soft themes up to +6). Only verified provider fields count as transport and stay evidence. Unmeasured hotel quietness or food proximity receives no fit points, while the constraint and raw wording remain available to later stay/itinerary modules. Demo hotel metadata is labeled mock; Live hotels remain Demo. Currency conversion and price comparisons remain at the existing provider boundary.
